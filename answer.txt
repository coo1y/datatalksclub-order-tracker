6. What was the problem?
Answer: The express delivery date calculation tried to use a day that does not exist in that month.
order_detail() computed the estimate with placed_at.replace(day=placed_at.day + 2). express-1002
is seeded with created_at set to the last day of the previous month, so day+2 always overflows
past the end of that month (e.g. Aug 31 -> day 33), raising ValueError -> 500. Fixed by using
placed_at + timedelta(days=2) instead.
